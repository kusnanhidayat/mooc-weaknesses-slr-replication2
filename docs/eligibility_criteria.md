# Eligibility Criteria (Table 1, PRISMA 2020 item 5)

| # | Inclusion | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary empirical studies that report weaknesses, problems, or limitations of MOOCs as experienced by or reported from MOOC participants | Studies that do not address MOOCs, or that discuss MOOCs without reporting weaknesses from the participants' perspective |
| 2 | Indexed in the Scopus database | Not indexed in the Scopus database |
| 3 | Full text accessible in PDF format | Full text not obtainable |
| 4 | Peer-reviewed journal article published as a complete empirical report | Review articles, meta-analyses, conceptual, opinion, or SWOT-literature papers, editorials, retracted articles, conference proceedings, book chapters, and non-peer-reviewed sources |
| 5 | Any primary empirical research design that reports participant-derived data (quantitative, qualitative, or mixed methods) | Studies reporting only provider-, institution-, content-, or platform-side evaluations without data collected from participants |

## Selection procedure

Screening and eligibility decisions were made by the first author and
independently checked by the second author; disagreements were resolved by
discussion. The decision and reason for every one of the 26 records are in
columns `decision` and `exclusion_reason` of `data/processed/appendix_A_records.csv`.
